## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Moo Who?**. This trainer adds configurable gameplay assistance for private matches and local testing. It focuses on movement, animal visibility, fly alerts, sound-pressure timing, and the hunter's limited ammunition while preserving the game's core voice-mimicry and deception mechanics.

**Target Audience:** Players testing private lobbies, content creators preparing demonstrations, and users who want adjustable movement, alert, and hunter-shot settings while practicing Moo Who?'s deception gameplay.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Start the trainer after launching the game and enable movement assistance only after entering a match. Adjust the speed gradually to avoid losing control while moving through animal herds. Normal movement remains important because suspicious positioning can reveal the human-controlled animal even when voice mimicry is convincing.

### Tools & Inventory

Moo Who? does not use a conventional player inventory. Animal players rely on movement, camouflage, microphone-based animal sounds, and timing. The hunter's shotgun is the primary limited resource, and its ammunition controls are available under the Combat section.

### World & Environment

Matches take place on a farm where human-controlled animals blend into NPC herds. Use the trainer's movement and fly-alert options for private practice, then disable them to experience the intended hide-and-survive loop. Continue making the assigned animal sound when prompted, because silence can trigger flies and make the player easier for hunters to locate.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is centered on the hunter's shotgun and careful target selection. Hunters should observe herd movement and listen for suspicious sounds before firing, because missed shots waste limited opportunities. The ammunition controls can restore shots for testing, while shot protection can reduce penalties during practice.
